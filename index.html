I'm Mike Partee. I've written music since 2009.

I wanted a fun way to share music with friends. Releasing on platforms felt too formal, and slinging mp3s around felt too casual.

This has become my home for all the songs I've written over the years. Some tracks are finished, some are just ideas.

I hope you have fun discovering what surprises hide in this stack!
